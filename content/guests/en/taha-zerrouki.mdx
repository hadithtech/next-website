# Taha Zerrouki

Professor Taha Zerrouki is a computer science professor from Algeria working on Arabic natural language processing. He builds open source software for the Arabic language, including Mishkal for diacritizing text, Qutrub for verb conjugation, the free Arabic spellchecker, and Alfanous, a Quran search engine.
