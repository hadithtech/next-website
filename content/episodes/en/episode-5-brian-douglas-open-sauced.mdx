# Episode 5: Open Sauced with Brian Douglas

Brian Douglas (bdougie), founder of OpenSauced, joins us to talk about his journey into tech and how developers can get the most out of open source.

## Topics Covered

- How Brian got into coding
- Empowering minorities and why diversity in tech is a strength
- Managing a remote company
- How to start in open source and use it to get a job
- Should open source make money?
- Online open source resumes and the future of open source
- Advice for people getting into open source

## About the Guest

Brian Douglas is the founder of OpenSauced and a long-time developer advocate passionate about open source communities.
