# Episode 6: Machine Learning Unified (Ivy) with Daniel Lenton

Daniel Lenton, founder of Ivy (now Unify), talks about moving from a PhD to founding a company and his vision for unifying machine learning frameworks.

## Topics Covered

- How Daniel got into coding
- The connection between psychology and computer science
- Moving from a PhD to being a founder
- Unifying machine learning frameworks with Ivy
- The YC story, support networks and team management
- What he looks for when hiring
- The future of machine learning and the impact of open source on Ivy

## About the Guest

Daniel Lenton is the founder of Unify (formerly Ivy), an open source project that unifies machine learning frameworks.
